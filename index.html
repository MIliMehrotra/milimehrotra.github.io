About my work

Connecting operational decisions with social outcomes.

My research examines how organizations can coordinate decisions and design incentives to achieve broader social objectives. I study these questions in supply chains, healthcare, and resource allocation, using mathematical modeling and optimization.

Across these settings, I am interested in how the actions of individual stakeholders affect the performance of the wider system.
